Who We Are

About Us

We believe the earth reveals its deepest serenity and ancient wonder only after dusk. Founded in 2021 by a collective of professional astronomers, wilderness guides, and nocturnal photographers, Night Trips crafts intimate journeys far away from light pollution.

Every expedition is planned around lunar cycles, celestial alignments, and zero-impact ecological principles, offering you unforgettable front-row seats to the cosmos in utmost comfort and safety.
